About Andre Kadeem

Grounded London rap shaped by composure, clear observation, self-possession and direct street perspective.

Andre Kadeem represents a measured London rap lane within NextGen Sessions, using controlled delivery and clear observation rather than exaggerated performance.

Don’t Watch That centres on self-possession and refusing to let outside attention dictate movement. His current public catalogue is focused, supported by a consistent night-time London visual identity and a calm, direct presence.
