Privacy

Clear data. No hidden tracking.

This notice explains the limited information used to understand website performance and review artist submissions.

Cookie-free site analytics

NextGen Sessions uses a first-party analytics endpoint hosted with the website. It does not set analytics cookies, create visitor IDs, use advertising pixels or fingerprint devices.

The analytics dataset stores only:

  • The page path visited.
  • A broad action type, such as opening an artist profile, playing a release or using a catalogue filter.
  • A limited content label, such as an artist slug, YouTube video ID or genre filter.

Typed search terms, form contents, email addresses, listening links, IP addresses and user-agent strings are not written to the analytics dataset. Analytics records are retained by Cloudflare Workers Analytics Engine for up to three months.

Artist submissions

Submission details are used to review the track and contact the artist if there is a suitable opportunity. The form is processed by FormSubmit and sent to the NextGen Sessions contact inbox. Do not include sensitive personal information.

YouTube and external links

The homepage YouTube player connects to YouTube only after a visitor presses play. Links to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, SoundCloud or other external services are governed by those services’ own privacy terms.

Questions or deletion requests

To ask about this notice or request deletion of an artist submission, email NextGen Sessions. Aggregate site events contain no visitor identifier, so they cannot be matched back to a specific person.
