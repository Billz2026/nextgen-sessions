About Jay Starks

Queens-rooted hip-hop built from neighbourhood memory, Jamaica Avenue perspective, ambition and long-term legacy.

Jay Starks represents the Queens storytelling lane within NextGen Sessions, pairing classic New York perspective with soulful production and grounded writing about identity, ambition and the neighbourhoods that shaped him.

Queens in My Soul, Jamaica Avenue, Architect of Havoc and No Shade In Queens form a connected catalogue where Queens is more than scenery. The writing moves through local memory, corner politics, neighbourhood pride and the question of what remains after the immediate moment passes.
