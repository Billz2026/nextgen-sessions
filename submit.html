Instagram submission

DM @next.gensessions

@next.gensessions

Keep it focused. One strong, finished track is more useful than a folder of unfinished music.

Include these seven details:

  1. Artist name
  2. Track title
  3. Genre
  4. City / country
  5. Your main social handle
  6. Listening link — YouTube, SoundCloud or another accessible link
  7. One sentence explaining why this track represents you
Important: Send a link, not a large audio attachment. Check that the link opens without requesting access.

Before sending

What we consider

  • Original music you are authorised to submit
  • Finished, clean audio
  • A clear artist identity
  • Music suited to the NextGen catalogue
  • Strong visual or campaign potential

What happens next

Simple and honest

NextGen Sessions reviews suitable submissions and replies where there is a genuine fit. Sending a track does not guarantee feedback, placement or release.
