About Reiss

Focused UK rap built around discipline, earned progress, routine and the refusal to expect shortcuts.

Reiss represents a focused UK rap lane within NextGen Sessions, favouring concise writing, direct delivery and records grounded in routine, self-reliance and steady progress rather than exaggerated claims.

No Comment, Already Know, On A Regular and On Route form the core of the Nothing Given project. Together, the four tracks present a consistent identity centred on doing the work, maintaining momentum and earning each step without expecting shortcuts.
