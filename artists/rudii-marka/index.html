About Rudii Marka

Cinematic Jamaican gully dancehall shaped by pressure, survival, loyalty and controlled melodic menace.

Rudii Marka represents the darker cinematic side of the NextGen Sessions dancehall roster, pairing gritty Jamaican street storytelling with melodic delivery and a composed visual presence.

Marked Rudeboi establishes his identity through pressure, loyalty and consequence, supported by rain-soaked gully imagery, controlled intensity and a recognisable long-dreadlocked silhouette. The presentation stays deliberate rather than chaotic, giving the record weight without relying on empty aggression.
