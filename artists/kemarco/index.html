About Kemarco

Kemarco is a Jamaican dancehall artist on NextGen Sessions, pairing dark melodic gully production with disciplined, controlled delivery.

Kemarco is a Jamaican dancehall artist from Jamaica and part of the NextGen Sessions dancehall roster. His sound centres on dark melodic gully production, low-register delivery and calm authority rather than reckless reaction.

His verified NextGen Sessions catalogue includes Ghetto Blessings and Badman Don’t Rush, with records built around patience, survival, discipline and controlled street pressure.
