About Reeko

Melodic Jamaican dancehall built around gullyside authority, nightlife energy, loyalty and sharp street observation.

Reeko represents the melodic gully dancehall lane within NextGen Sessions, balancing direct Jamaican delivery and street authority with hooks designed to carry beyond the first listen.

The Mi Call Di Shots album established his core identity through leadership, survival and loyalty. Nuff Man A Watch, After Di Shots and Smile Wid Knife then extended the catalogue into suspicion, consequence and watchful street perspective without losing the dark visual world and controlled confidence that define him.
