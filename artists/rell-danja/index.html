About Rell Danja

Hard-edged Jamaican dancehall built around betrayal, survival, loyalty, sleepless pressure and consequences.

Rell Danja represents a hard-edged Jamaican dancehall lane within NextGen Sessions, using direct writing and controlled delivery to explore survival, loyalty, pressure and the consequences attached to street decisions.

His current catalogue moves from betrayal and retaliation on Cross Me, Regret It to sleepless tension on No Sleep Fi Di Wicked and isolation on Nuh Friend In The Street. Together, the three records establish a consistent dark, gullyside identity without relying on exaggerated claims.
