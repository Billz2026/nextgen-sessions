About Omari V

Warm Jamaican lovers rock and reggae shaped by memory, distance, changing seasons and emotional restraint.

Omari V represents the reflective lovers-rock lane within NextGen Sessions, pairing warm Jamaican reggae production with calm, emotionally grounded writing.

His catalogue moves through love, separation, fading connection and seasonal memory without forcing the emotion. When Di Breeze Call brings the strongest current expression of that identity, while Whispers in the Wind, Yuh Gone Already and No Fire In Your Eyes establish a consistent, understated sound.
