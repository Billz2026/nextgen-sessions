About Arif Sial

A mature Punjabi and Saraiki folk voice with the raw emotion of a live mehfil and a qawwali-inspired performance style.

Arif Sial brings an earthy, commanding vocal character to NextGen Sessions, drawing on Punjabi and Saraiki folk storytelling and the communal energy of a traditional mehfil.

His sound centres expressive, weathered vocals, harmonium, dholak, handclaps and audience-style responses. His official recordings and latest releases are collected on this page as they are published.
