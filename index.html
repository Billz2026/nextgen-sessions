About NextGen

More than a submission page.

NextGen Sessions is an independent music network developing original artist identities, releases and visual campaigns across several genre lanes.

The platform is designed to give every release a clear concept, recognisable visual identity and a consistent home on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and the wider web.

What the platform builds

  • Original songs and recurring artists
  • Cinematic cover art and thumbnails
  • Full-release YouTube presentation
  • Short-form launch campaigns
  • A growing cross-genre catalogue
